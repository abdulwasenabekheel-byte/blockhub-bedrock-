HOW TO JOIN

Connect from Bedrock

  1. Open Minecraft Bedrock and choose Play → Servers.
  2. Choose Add Server (if available on your device).
  3. Enter the server name, address and port shown in a listing.
  4. Save and join. The server must be online and allow your account to connect.

Console platforms may restrict adding custom servers. This website cannot force Minecraft to open or bypass platform restrictions.
